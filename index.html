[PDF] The impact of social media on the mental health of adolescents and young adults: a systematic review

[PDF] cureus.com
AM Khalaf, AA Alubied, AM Khalaf, AA Rifaey… - Cureus, 2023 - cureus.com
Indicates that risks associated with social media usage for this population include privacy concerns, cyberbullying, and negative effects on schooling and mental health. Notes that data from several studies suggest social media use among teenagers correlates with an increase in mental distress, while ethical use can boost self-esteem and connection.

[HTML] mdpi.com
N Agyapong-Opoku, F Agyapong-Opoku… - Behavioral …, 2025 - mdpi.com
Indicates that the majority of studies reviewed linked social media use to adverse mental health outcomes in adolescents, especially depression and anxiety.
